# 📊 Flipkart Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Flipkart sales data to generate meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps understand revenue trends, profitability, customer behavior, and discount impact.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and profit performance
- Identify top-performing categories and regions
- Study customer segments and payment behavior
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit margin
- Track monthly revenue trends

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🟣 1. Executive Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Rating
- Profit Margin %
- Revenue by Category
- Profit by Region
- Monthly Revenue Trend

### 🟣 2. Customer & Payment Analysis
- Orders by Payment Method
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Average Rating by Category
- Top 10 Products by Revenue

### 🟣 3. Profit & Discount Analysis
- Discount vs Profit (Scatter Analysis)
- Profit Margin by Category
